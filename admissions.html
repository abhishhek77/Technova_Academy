    Admission Information

    Admissions are open throughout the year for new batches starting each month. Morning, evening and weekend batches are available.

    Registration Process

    1. Enquire

      Submit the enquiry form or visit the office.

    2. Counselling

      Meet our counsellor to choose the right course and batch.

    3. Register

      Fill the registration form and submit documents.

    4. Pay fee

      Pay the registration fee to confirm your seat.

    5. Start learning

      Attend the orientation and begin your classes.

    Required Documents

    • Passport-size photographs (2)
    • Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN or similar)
    • Latest educational certificates
    • Address proof
    • Completed registration form
